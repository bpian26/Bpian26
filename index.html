Executive summary

Vision at a Glance

BPIAN-26 represents a generation shaped by the values, traditions and enduring legacy of Panchagarh B.P. Government High School. More than a graduating batch, BPIAN-26 is a community built upon shared experiences, lifelong friendships, and a collective commitment to carry forward the spirit of our institution.

Our journey extends beyond the years spent in classrooms and school grounds. It is about the memories we created, the relationships we formed, the lessons we learned and the aspirations we carry into the future. Through this platform, BPIAN-26 brings together our seniors, batchmates, juniors and others to remain connected, celebrate achievements, showcase initiatives, meaningful events and preserve the moments that define our school life.

With unity, creativity, leadership and excellence at its core, BPIAN-26 strives to build a lasting community that continues to grow beyond graduation. It is a space where our shared past meets our future ambitions, a digital home for our batch and a lasting reflection of the legacy we proudly carry.

Rooted in our legacy. United by our journey. Inspired to shape what comes next.

Batch Highlights & Initiatives

BPIAN School Cricket League (BSCL)

The BPIAN School Cricket League (BSCL) 2025, organized by BPIAN-26, was an exciting cricket tournament that brought together students and teachers in a spirit of sportsmanship.

BSCL created an engaging platform where teachers and students shared the same field, competing for a single championship and strengthening the bond within the school community.

Organized entirely by BPIAN-26, the league featured structured group stages, high-voltage knockout rounds, and official match management. The tournament transformed the school grounds into a festive arena, drawing crowds of passionate supporters, vibrant commentary, and memorable night-lighting fixtures.

Beyond runs and wickets, BSCL set a new benchmark for student-led initiatives at Panchagarh B.P. Government High School—proving that sports can serve as a powerful bridge for mentorship, teamwork, and lasting school spirit.

BPIAN-26 Rag Day Celebration

The Rag Day of BPIAN-26 was a memorable event, organized to celebrate the final years of school life with our beloved friends and respected teachers.

The celebration featured commemorative T-shirts, photography sessions, and a live concert featuring performances by Sohan Ali and Band Biborton.
